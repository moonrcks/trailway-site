Trailway Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Trailway (“the app”) plans bike routes on Ohio's bike paths and records your rides. This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your device, and the choices you have. Trailway has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer does not run its own server.

Stored on your iPhone

Your rides (GPS track, times, speed, elevation, heart rate and calories), saved routes, profile details (including date of birth, height, weight and emergency contact), bikes and gear are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. Your password is stored only as a salted hash in the iPhone Keychain. Deleting the app deletes this information.

Apple Health and Apple Watch

With your permission, Trailway saves rides to Apple Health as cycling workouts and reads heart rate, workouts and routes to show them in your rides and to import past rides. Health data stays on your devices. It is never posted to Social, shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising or marketing.

Location

Your location is used to plan routes from where you are and to record rides. During a ride, tracking continues with the screen locked and stops when the ride ends. Location is not sent anywhere except as described below for route planning, and as part of a ride you choose to share.

Social (only what you share)

Social features use Trailway's public iCloud (CloudKit) database, operated by Apple. The following is stored there and visible to other Trailway riders:

WhatWhen
UsernameAlways, so usernames stay unique
Profile: display name, photo, bio, hometown, riding style, ride and mile totalsUnless you set your profile to “Only me”
Who you followWhen you follow someone
A shared ride: title, date, distance, time, speed, elevation, calories, caption, up to 4 photos, and the route (with the first and last 300 m hidden if you choose, or no map at all)Only when you share that ride
Kudos, comments and reportsWhen you post them
Direct messagesWhen you send them. Messages are end-to-end encrypted on your iPhone: only you and the rider you write to can read them. If you report a message, its text is sent with the report so it can be reviewed.

With Post automatically on (Settings → Social & privacy), each ride you record over a quarter mile is shared when it ends, with the first and last 300 m of the route hidden. Choose Ask each time or Don't post to share rides only when you decide to.

Heart rate and other Health data are never included in shared rides. Your email address is never shared.

Services the app contacts

  • OpenStreetMap (Overpass API) receives a map area to download local streets and trails for route planning.
  • Apple Maps receives points along a route to fill in street directions and place searches.
  • National Weather Service, Iowa Environmental Mesonet (radar tiles), USGS (topographic tiles) and Ohio DOT OHGO (traffic cameras and road work) receive the map area you're viewing.
  • Apple Music, only if you allow it: Trailway reads what's playing during a ride to show it, control playback, and save the song list with the ride on your iPhone.
  • Spotify, only if you connect it: Trailway reads what's playing and your recently played songs during rides, and sends play, pause and skip commands. Song lists stay with your rides on your iPhone.
  • Strava, only if you connect it: rides upload to your own Strava account and past rides are downloaded from it. You can disconnect at any time in Settings.
  • Sign in with Apple or Google, only if you choose them to sign in. Trailway receives an account identifier and, if you allow it, your email address, which stay on your device.

None of these services receive your name, your rides or your Health data from Trailway, except Strava when you connect it.

Your choices

  • Hide your profile from Social: Settings → Social & privacy → Only me.
  • Delete a shared ride: open it and choose Delete.
  • Block or report a rider or post from its menu.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your account, your Social profile, shared rides, follows, kudos, comments and sent messages, and frees your username.
  • Export your rides as GPX files: Settings → Data & storage.

Children

Trailway is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from them.
